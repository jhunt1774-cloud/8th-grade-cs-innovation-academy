CS Innovation Academy

Ohio Grade 8 computer science. Nine weeks. Forty minutes per day. Entirely computer-based.

Start with Week 1

  1. Open the complete student website at Week 1.
  2. Open the Week 1 teaching guide and sample responses.
  3. Present the Week 1 browser slide deck.
  4. Copy Lesson 1 Classroom instructions and student response template.
  5. Review the Lesson 1 assessment PDF and private answer key.
  6. Follow the account import and Classroom checklist. Account integration is not tested.

The complete course

  • 45 student lessons with readings, worked examples, labs, vocabulary practice and response exports
  • Nine-week plan and all 46 source-standard mappings
  • Teacher guides for all 45 lessons with pacing, differentiation, exemplars and rubrics
  • Keys for 45 daily checks, nine unit tests, benchmark and final
  • Classroom assignment manifest
  • Checks performed and remaining verification limits

Readings contain 502–596 words per lesson, including shared unit reference instruction. Assessments contain 306 unique questions in 705 placements. Some items intentionally recur. Slides are browser-native HTML, and Classroom packages are ready-to-copy text rather than live posted assignments.
